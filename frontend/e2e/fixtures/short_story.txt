The Ember Crown

Chapter One

In the northern keep of Valdris, the last heir of the crown woke to the sound of horns. Mara had trained all her life for this day—the day the old king would name his successor. She pulled on her father's cloak, the one with the ember sigil, and walked into the great hall. The lords were already assembled. What they did not know was that the true enemy was not beyond the walls, but within.
